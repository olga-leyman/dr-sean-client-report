What we changed last Monday

After you said the familiar, high-intent patients are the ones you want more of, we stopped spreading budget across every procedure and pointed it at name + rhinoplasty.

Google Search

Paused Breast Lift, Male Rhinoplasty, and Competitor. Raised Brand Search from $5 to $25 a day and Exact rhinoplasty from $10 to $20. Those two campaigns are how someone who already knows him — or just watched a Reel — finds the practice when they type a name or “rhinoplasty.”

Meta

Paused the national quiz outside California (it was spending without producing local requests) and leftover 2025 lead-gen. Raised rhinoplasty Reels from $20 to $50 a day so more people actually see him. Raised the Southern California quiz from $50 to $80.
