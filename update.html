How we will judge it

Review around October 13. $30/day each. Primary measure: cost per completed request, with a working target of about $300 — in range of the consultation fee. We will also watch request volume, lead quality, and booked consultations.

How to read it

The pages run in separate campaigns, so traffic differences will be considered. If inquiry volume is still thin, we extend the window rather than call a winner early. Fourteen days from today’s creative and budget is the first fair checkpoint — not the eight days that already proved reach.

Next reporting priorities

  • Cost per request by campaign and landing page, against the ~$300 working target.
