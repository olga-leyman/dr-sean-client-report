Google Search is filling the consultation pipeline.

A client performance story for the Beverly Hills practice. Meta introduced Dr. Sean to 235,862 people. Patients then searched the name on Google, clicked through, and requested a consultation.

The patient path: Meta introduces. Google closes.

This is the journey we built. A patient sees Dr. Sean on Instagram or Facebook. She may never click that ad. Later she Googles the name. Brand Search is sitting on “dr sean plastic surgery” and “dr sean saadat,” so the consult is captured on Google — at $158 CPA.

We checked the path. Here is what the data can prove.

We looked for the exact sequence: saw a Reel, visited the site, Googled Dr. Sean, filled in the form. Meta, Instagram, Google Ads, and the website do not share one person ID, so we cannot name a single viewer and walk her through all four steps. We can prove each step of the system — and that is the right proof for this journey, because most people never click the Reel.

CheckWhat we foundWhat it means
Did Meta actually reach people? 235,862 unique people. 310,478 video views. Reels campaign: 135,042 people. The name was introduced at scale. June is when it turned on (3.5k → 74k people).
Did anyone go to the website after Meta? Yes. ~4,900 Paid Social users on the site. 9,540 Meta landing-page views. 10 started a consult form. Clicks from Meta do land. Instagram in-app also sent 5 completed website forms.
Did they then search the name? Google clicks on Dr. Sean / Saadat terms: 12 in May → 18 in June → 27 in August, Meta’s biggest reach month. Name-search demand rose after Reels scaled. That is the view-through path in aggregate.

What this season proved

Patients in Los Angeles already search by procedure and by surgeon name. We built Google Search around that behavior — brand, exact rhinoplasty, consultation intent, and male rhinoplasty — and it is doing the job. Website consultation requests arrived every month from May through September. Click-through held between 5% and 6%, which is strong for competitive Beverly Hills plastic surgery auctions.

Brand is working

Searches for Dr. Sean’s name are converting. Reputation and Search are reinforcing each other: patients who already know the practice can find it instantly, and that traffic is not being handed to a competitor.

Rhinoplasty is the engine

The live rhinoplasty page is the strongest Google destination. Homepage and gallery also convert. That matches how Beverly Hills patients shop — procedure first, proof second, then a consult.

The pipeline behind the ads

Across May–September the website recorded 73 consultation requests. Forty came from Paid Search. Organic search and organic social added 18 more at no media cost. The front desk worked inquiries through the summer, including a June inquiry that progressed to surgery in September.
