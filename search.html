rhinoplasty 90,500 / month. rhinoplasty near me 5,400. male rhinoplasty 2,900. City modifiers are how local patients narrow that. “Near me” is the workhorse.

His name

What this means

  • Do not build a West Hollywood Search campaign. Ten searches a month. The practice already covers that geography from Beverly Hills.
  • Keep paid on rhinoplasty near me, rhinoplasty beverly hills, and rhinoplasty los angeles. That is the real local demand.
  • Add Brand negatives for alemi, doherty, satey, weiss, simon, and “david saadat” so name budget stays on this practice.
  • SEO should rank for LA and Beverly Hills rhinoplasty first. A WeHo URL is last, not first. Search Console is not connected yet — once it is, we can add the organic query table (position, clicks, impressions).
